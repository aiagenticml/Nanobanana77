## 2. UGC (User Generated Content) Environments

```text
UGC-style rear camera iPhone video pointed at a bathroom mirror, woman holding the phone partially covering her face, messy bathroom background with open toothpaste tube and a few scattered towels, toothpaste smudges on the mirror.
```

**Kitchen Vlog**
```text
Transform the reference character into a photorealistic casual smartphone capture filmed while standing in a domestic kitchen... include mid-shelf clutter and natural lighting.
```

**Home Workspace (Highly Detailed)**
```text
Transform the reference character into a photorealistic casual creator-style smartphone capture seated at a desk in a natural home workspace environment. The output must resemble a raw camera roll image and must NOT contain any platform UI or graphical overlays. The provided product sits on the desk and is actively interacted with using natural hand gestures. Lighting includes mixed cool screen glow and warm ambient practical lighting creating realistic color temperature inconsistency. Framing is chest-up with slight asymmetry and mild handheld tilt. Background must include believable desk clutter such as cables, packaging, notebooks, and personal objects creating environmental authenticity. No AI smoothness, no cinematic studio lighting.
```
