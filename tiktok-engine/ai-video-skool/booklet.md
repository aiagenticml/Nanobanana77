# 🛸 AI Video Skool: The Ultimate AI Video Booklet

## 📘 Chapter 2: Image — The Foundation
Image generation is the most critical part of the video workflow. High-quality video starts with a high-quality, controlled image.

### 🛠️ Key Model: Nano Banana Pro
Available inside **Higgsfield**, this is the primary model for all high-fidelity image generations.
*   **Basic Process:** Reference Image(s) → Prompt → Generate.

### 💡 Core Lessons & Tips
*   **The "Power of Imperfection":** AI often creates "too perfect" images which looks fake. To achieve photorealism, always prompt for natural imperfections: *skin pores, slight asymmetry, messy hair, oily sheen, or tired eyes.*
*   **Identity Anchor Kit:** To maintain character consistency across multiple scenes, first create a "Master Asset" (a high-detail portrait) and then use it as a **Face Reference** for all subsequent generations.
*   **Training Taste:** Success is 50% tool knowledge and 50% "Taste." Look at high-fashion editorial campaigns (Pinterest) and try to match them 1:1 using AI.

### 📜 Featured Prompts: Chapter 2
*   **Base Character (Subway Woman):** "Photorealistic candid photograph of a woman sitting on a subway train during rush hour, slightly slouched posture, tired expression, faint dark circles under eyes, visible pores and natural skin texture, small blemishes on chin, oily skin sheen on forehead, smudged mascara, flyaway hairs, no CGI, no illustration."
*   **The Boxer:** "Photorealistic candid photograph of a male boxer sitting in the corner of a boxing ring during training... elbows resting on his knees, shoulders slightly slouched from exhaustion, a healed scar subtly visible under his left eyebrow... old boxing gloves showing heavy wear."
*   **Outpainting Tip:** "Extend the provided image to a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio using seamless outpainting."

## 🎬 Chapter 3: Video — Bringing Scenes to Life
Once your master image is ready, use video models to add movement and emotion.

### 🛠️ Key Model: Kling 3.0
The primary recommendation for high-quality motion and lip-syncing.
*   **Motion Control:** Use "Elements" or "Motion Brushes" to specify which parts of the image should move (e.g., hair blowing vs. background staying still).

### 💡 Core Lessons & Tips
*   **Camera Movement = Emotion:**
    *   **Dolly In:** Focus, intensity, tension.
    *   **Dolly Out:** Isolation, scale, loneliness.
    *   **Pan:** Observation, scanning the scene.
*   **Lip Syncing:** Use **ElevenLabs** for voice cloning and then pair it with Kling's mouth-sync features for talking-head videos.

### 📜 Featured Prompts: Chapter 3
*   **Slow Motion Action:** "The main character is slowly blinking and looks directly at the camera with a subtle, friendly smile... he remains perfectly still throughout the video, his expression doesn't change and the background remains stationary."
*   **Rolex Commercial:** "The rolex watch is floating and glowing as gold dust swirls around it. Gold particles drift towards the camera. One bright lens flare flashes over the face of the watch and disappears. High detailed 4k cinematic ad."

## ⚡ Chapter 4: Workflow Breakdowns
Putting it all together into specific products.

### 💡 The AI Influencer Workflow
1.  **Identity Merge:** Use **Soul Cinema** or **Nano Banana Pro** with 3 reference faces from Pinterest to create a 100% unique "AI Influencer" face.
2.  **Generate a Portfolio:** Use the **Identity Anchor Kit** prompts (Front, Side, Smile, Full Body) to lock in the character.
3.  **UGC Scenes:** Place the character in "real-life" settings (Cafe, Park, Kitchen) using **Face Reference**.

### 📜 Featured Prompts: Chapter 4
*   **Identity Fusion:** "Use all three attached reference images as identity references to merge their features into this new identity. A high detailed closeup photo of a young woman, looking directly at the camera with a friendly smile, authentic skin texture."
*   **Master Asset Generation:** "The character in the first image is wearing the exact outfit from the second and third images... preserve her identity, facial features, and bone structure... full body shot that serves as a master reference."
