### Post 4 — "The Meeting Vent Trick"
**Hook:** *"Use AI to get out of your own head after a bad meeting"*
**Slide flow:**
1. "Frustrated after a meeting? Don't just vent to a friend."
2. "Voice-record yourself venting for 2 minutes"
3. "Transcribe it (use Whisper — free)"
4. "Paste into Claude: 'Point out any cognitive biases or distortions in what I said'"
5. "You'll see the situation completely differently"
6. "This is free therapy. Use it."
**Emotion:** Relatable + Surprising

### Post 6 — "The First Draft Rule"
**Hook:** *"Stop treating ChatGPT like Google. Do this instead."*
**Slide flow:**
1. "Most people use AI wrong"
2. "They ask once, get an okay answer, and stop"
3. "Pro move: treat every AI response as a FIRST DRAFT"
4. "Follow up: 'Make this 50% shorter' / 'Add more examples' / 'What are the downsides?'"
5. "Iteration = elite output. The magic is in round 2."
**Emotion:** Educational + Empowering
